Many visitors never see your homepage. They arrive on a practice-area page, a location page, or a blog post straight from a search. Wherever they land, the way to reach the firm should be in plain sight.

## Put it in the header

The simplest fix is to show the phone number in the site header, on every page. On a phone, the header often shrinks, so keep at least a tappable phone icon or a short "Call" button there.

Write the number as text, not as part of an image. Text can be tapped, copied, and read by screen readers.

## Make it tappable

On a phone, a number should start a call when tapped. That takes a `tel:` link. Most website builders do this when you mark the number as a phone link. Test it on your own phone.

## Give the contact page a clear name

Use a plain label in the menu, such as "Contact." Visitors look for that word. Clever labels make them hunt.

The contact page itself should show:

- The phone number
- A short contact form
- The office address, if you meet people there
- Office hours
- What happens after someone reaches out

## End each page with a next step

At the bottom of practice-area pages and blog posts, add a short line that points to the phone number or the contact page. "If you'd like to talk this through, call the office or send a message" is enough. It catches the reader who has just finished and is ready to act.

## Keep the details the same everywhere

The phone number in the header, the footer, the contact page, and the Google Business Profile should all match. If the firm uses a tracking number on some pages, make sure it rings the same office and that the main number still appears where people expect it.

## Check it from a visitor's view

Pick three pages that are not the homepage. Open each one on a phone. Without scrolling to the bottom, can you find the number and call it? Can you get to the contact form in one tap? If either answer is no, that page needs a small fix.

None of this is complicated. It is just easy to overlook, because the people who maintain the site already know the number by heart.
