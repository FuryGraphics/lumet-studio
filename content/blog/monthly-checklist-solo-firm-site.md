A firm site does not need constant attention, but it does need regular attention. Details drift. A phone extension changes, an office moves, a form stops sending. A short monthly check catches these before a visitor does.

Set a recurring reminder for the same day each month. Most months, this takes well under an hour.

## Contact details

1. Tap the phone number on your phone. Does it call the office?
2. Send a test message through the contact form. Did it arrive where someone will see it?
3. Check the address, hours, and phone number in the footer and on the contact page.

## Google Business Profile

4. Open your [Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/). Confirm the hours, phone number, and website link.
5. Look for any edits Google or the public has suggested, and accept or reject them.
6. Read new reviews and respond briefly where it makes sense, keeping confidentiality in mind.
7. Set holiday hours for the coming month.

## Pages

8. Skim the homepage and the main practice-area pages. Is anything out of date?
9. Check the about page. Are the people, photos, and bar admissions current?
10. Click a few links in the menu and footer. Do any lead to a missing page?

## On a phone

11. Open the homepage, a practice-area page, and the contact page on your phone. Can you read them and tap through without trouble?

## Rules and wording

12. If anything was added this month, read it once more against your state's attorney advertising rules. Look for promises about outcomes or comparisons with other firms.

## The technical basics

13. Confirm the site loads over a secure connection, with the padlock showing in the browser.
14. If the site runs on a platform that needs updates, make sure they were applied or ask whoever maintains it.
15. Confirm the domain name and hosting are set to renew, and that the contact email on those accounts still works.

## Write it down

Keep a short note of what you checked and anything you changed. It makes the next month faster and helps if someone else takes over the site later.

A few minutes each month keeps the site accurate, which is most of what visitors need from it.
